# Voice and editorial dictionary

## The writing sequence
What happened → what's noteworthy → what supports it → what it does **not** establish → where to explore.

## Vocabulary (how every number is labelled)
Each claim on the site is one of these kinds. Name the kind whenever a reader could mistake it for another.

| Kind | Meaning | Examples on the site | Display rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Official** | Recorded by the NHL | final score, standings points, goals, assists, box score | Shown first and never qualified away. Nothing a model says overrides it. |
| **Observed** | Measured by us from official event data | shot attempts, ice time, who was on the ice, PDO, faceoff results | Plain statement with source and date. |
| **Expected / estimated** | A model's estimate of something that happened | xG, GSAx, WAR-lite, ratings, "deserved to win", points from the chances | Say *expected* or *estimated*, name the model, and give a range when one exists. Never "should have", "real score" or "deserved" in our own voice. |
| **Projected** | A forecast of something that hasn't happened | projected standings, playoff and Cup odds, player projections | Probability or range, with the as-of date; "projected", not "will". |
| **Hypothetical** | A what-if built on our rules | trade explorer, trade playoff odds, GM moves, the shot replay | Say it's a scenario. Trade verdicts are a pricing rule, not a real team's acceptance. |
| **Inferred** | Our reading of indirect evidence | team phase, man-games lost (absence proxy), availability tiers | Label "inferred" or "proxy"; show what it's inferred from. |
| **Reported** | Someone else said it | trade-block reports, signings news, Polymarket prices | Name the source and date; we don't vouch for it. |
| **Experimental** | Shadow, challenger or in-test models | roster-aware simulation, RAPM WAR | Label "experimental" or "shadow"; never present it as the published number. |

## Words to use
"by the chances", "5-on-5 expected goals", "the scoreboard and the chances disagreed", "shot-quality replay", "worth watching", "early-season sample", "the model estimates", "running hot / running cold" (describing the past).

## Words to avoid
"stolen win", "deserved to lose", "the real score", "guaranteed regression", "they're due", "the model knows", "AI-powered insights", "unlock powerful insights", "comprehensive analytics", and invented quotes, bylines, certainty or contrarianism.

## Copy direction (examples, not claims)
| Generic | Our direction |
|---|---|
| "Explore comprehensive hockey analytics" | "There's more to the game than the final score." |
| "Leverage advanced analytics to evaluate…" | "See what the chances, matchups and minutes say." |
| "Our model predicts the real winner" | Never ship. The result is the result. |
| "The team is due for a bounce-back" | "Its recent results trail its shot quality; that isn't a guarantee of what comes next." |
| "Trade accepted" | "This package passes Hockey Lab's pricing rule; real-world acceptance is unknown." |
| Loading… | "Pulling last night's numbers…" (concrete, short) |
| No data | Say what's missing and when it usually arrives. |
