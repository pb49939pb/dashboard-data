# Five-fan test (rollout section 7), for after Phase 2, 3 and 4

Three to five hockey fans, on their own phones, without explaining the design first. Pat runs it; record plain notes, look for repeated confusion, and don't compute statistics from five people.

**Setup for Phase 2:** the new masthead is owner-only, so hand them your phone signed in (Overview), or wait for Phase 3 and test both together.

## Tasks (say only this)
1. "Find the result of the latest completed game. Does the site agree with the final result?" (Right answer: the final score is a fact; the site's numbers are about how it happened.)
2. "Find one piece of evidence behind one of Today's headlines."
3. "From a game recap, get to the player or lineup details."

## Then ask
"Does this feel like an interesting independent hockey site? Is any part confusing, slow, or too self-important?"
