# The Other Score: brand thesis (working direction)

Status: an approved **working design direction** for Hockey Lab (patrickreidbaldwin.com/hockey), not yet a public rename. The original thesis document (`the-other-score-brand-thesis.md`) wasn't in this repo, so this file is adapted from the owner's rollout plan of 2026-10-14 (see `rollout.md`). When the full thesis is available, paste it here, because this file is the source of truth for the creative direction.

## The idea
- **Emotional premise:** the game is over; the conversation isn't.
- **Intellectual premise:** a final score is a fact. An explanation remains open to evidence.
- **Working strapline:** *The game has a final score. It doesn't have a final explanation.*
- **Short descriptor:** *Independent NHL analysis, projections, and game stories.*
- **Destination:** an independent hockey publication with an analytical workbench inside it. Hockey Lab grows into a point of view; it isn't replaced by a theme.

## Voice
A perceptive hockey fan with a research habit. The voice is curious and plain-spoken, and it shows its work. It never treats a model as the truth about a game, never claims certainty it doesn't have, and never sells. See `voice.md`.

## Design: ink, ice and evidence
- **Editorial hierarchy:** one dominant insight or task per view, then supporting evidence. Rules and rows are preferred over yet more cards.
- **Cool restraint:** a dark, inky base with a frost accent and a coral used sparingly for narrative emphasis. Team colors stay team colors.
- **Data is the motif:** chance comparisons, shot locations, game flow and annotations are the decoration.

Candidate palette (validate on phones before wider use; scoped only, never global at first):

| Role | Candidate | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Arena ink | `#0D1822` | deep background in branded regions |
| Slate panel | `#243747` | secondary surfaces |
| Scorebook paper | `#F2F0E9` | foreground / long-form |
| Rink frost | `#A3D8D4` | editorial links, analytical accent |
| Steel | `#A8BCC9` | secondary labels, metadata |
| Signal coral | `#F08065` | sparing highlights, narrative annotations |

Type: existing fonts first. A condensed display face (e.g. Barlow Condensed) and a reading face (e.g. Source Sans 3) are candidates to trial later, only if they earn their bytes on real phones.

## Non-negotiables (from the rollout plan)
Everything fans use stays where it is and works the same: scores, standings, recaps, every tab, the owner-only workbench, sources and dates, speed, deep links and the back button. Models, data paths and permissions are out of scope for any brand change. The name change itself waits for the Phase 7 go/no-go decision, after brand, domain and trademark clearance.
